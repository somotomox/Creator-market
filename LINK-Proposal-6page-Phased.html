What we found

What we found in the prototype

We reviewed the live build across roughly twenty-one screens (marketing, sign-in, browse, the creator profile and booking panel, settings, and the signed-in app). The verdict: stronger than a typical throwaway prototype, with a working booking surface, but four structural gaps stand between it and a trustworthy, revenue-generating product.

Already works well

  • Creator profile and booking panel (tiered offers, availability, trust footer)
  • Thorough settings, including Stripe payout configuration
  • Browse, categories, and solid content pages
  • A coherent coral visual identity

Four structural gaps

  • Critical No social sign-in or verified followers, the core concept
  • Critical Creator and fan experiences are mixed together
  • Critical Invented, inconsistent numbers (a legal risk)
  • High No creator onboarding or real dashboard; payments look unfinished

Our Phase 1 plan: keep, fix, and build

Keep
  • Booking flow and creator profile
  • Stripe-ready settings and payouts
  • Browse, categories, content pages
  • The coral visual identity
Fix
  • Remove the invented numbers and claims
  • Separate the creator and fan experiences
  • One landing page, one design system
  • Layout bugs and accessibility
Build
  • Social sign-in and verified followers
  • Live checkout with protected payments
  • Creator onboarding and dashboard
  • Reviews, reminders, and basic admin tools
Why Phase 1 is affordableBecause we build on the working prototype rather than starting from zero, the budget goes into a short list of high-value fixes and additions, not a rebuild. That is what makes six weeks and $8,000 to $10,000 realistic, and it is why the pilot codebase carries straight into Phase 2 instead of being replaced.
