05

What moves these dates

Late dependencies move the week, they do not compress it

Anything in a Needed line above that arrives more than three working days late moves that week's milestone rather than shortening the work, and we confirm that in writing in the same week. It is not a penalty, it is what keeps a fixed fee against a fixed duration honest for both sides.

The outer bound

Six weeks is the plan and the basis of the fee. The client has said that quality matters more than speed and that running to roughly three months would be acceptable, which sets a hard outer bound of mid December 2026. That room exists to absorb late answers and research findings, not additional scope, which still follows change control.

Two decisions still sit on the critical path

The launch niche is tabled and blocks creator recruitment and every category-specific form. The Stripe entity carries multi-day verification inside it and gates the payments week. Both are Week 2 items, and both are the client's to answer.
